Description             : I've always loved The Plutonia Experiment, its
                          great difficulty jump compared to DOOM, DOOM II
                          And TNT mixed with the fantastic level design by
                          Milo & Dario Casali makes it my favourite of the
                          Original 4 IWADs.

                          So out of nowhere I decided, hey! Why not build
                          a small 6-7 Level campaign for Plutonia with that
                          Heavy, and I mean HEAVY inspiration from some of
                          My favourite levels from The Plutonia Experiment!

                          So about 2 weeks and 6 maps later I've finally
                          finished "Verloren!". If you wondered why its
                          called that its because Verloren is the German
                          word for "Lost", I was shoving random words in
                          a bunch of languages like; Greek, Italian, French
                          German etc and out of nowhere "Verloren" just hit
                          me. It felt like the perfect name. It sounds
                          Plutonia like in my opinion.

                          No real story to be honest, but I do want to add
                          though, this WAD is TOUGH AS NAILS on Ultra
                          Violence, But if you really want that extra
                          challenge I HIGHLY recommend pistol starting
                          every map. I did and I thought it was a blast!

                          Each map takes about 10-20 Minutes to complete
                          and don't have many secrets in them, so don't
                          worry about wall-humping for some soulsphere.
                          The secrets I did add aren't too hard to find.

                          Have fun!
Additional Credits to   : Dario & Milo Casali obviously because they made
                          The Plutonia Experiment. Specifically MAP01,
                          MAP02, MAP04, MAP05 and MAP16 were inspirations.
