Description             : This is a small, 2-4 player Deathmatch Map for
                          DOOM II that takes place in the void itself. 
                          The map has all weapons, but you will always end
                          up with a Chaingun or Single-Barrel at spawn. 
                          All of the other weapons like the Double-Barrel/
                          Super Shotgun, The Plasma Gun, Rocket Launcher
                          and even the BFG9000 can be found if you look
                          hard enough, but the other weapons mentioned
                          take quite the risk taking to acquire. You will
                          always be close to death, even if its not from
                          another person... Just be careful, ok?

                          Other than that, there is 1 Green Armour, 1 Blue
                          Armour, 1 Backpack, tons of Ammo and No health.
                          OH, and there is also a radioactive suit if you
                          stumble upon it ;). Have fun dudes!
