Title                   : ArchLiver's Spooky Halloween Special!
Filename                : 10_31_26.wad
Release date            : October 21st 2026
Author                  : ArchLiver
Email Address           : archdoomaddict@gmail.com
Other Files By Author   : Too many to count. Check my website if you know the
                          URL to it.
Misc. Author Info       : Spookiest Doom-Mapper.

Description             : This is a singular-map for DOOM II that as the
                          title implies: is Halloween Themed. This map takes
                          around 5-10 minutes to complete and is meant to be
                          a challenge, so, I'd recommend playing on lower
                          difficulties if scarce health pickups and high
                          enemy counts isn't your cup of tea. All weapons
                          except the BFG9000 & Chainsaw are included, I've
                          also included a new sky and my very own
                          jack-o-lantern sprites as a replacement for the
                          evil-eyes. This WAD was meant to be released on
                          Halloween, but I probably won't have my computer by
                          then as I have to send it away to get fixed real
                          soon, so I've chosen to release this map 10 days
                          early. Due to me being in extreme crunch-mode
                          trying to rush this map out of the door, a lot of
                          what I had in mind has been scrapped, so I
                          apologise for that. I've included a bunch of graves
                          for you to rob for ammo too, have fun!

Additional Credits to   : 3D-Realms I suppose, since I used the buildings
                          from Duke 3D's first sky texture for my own sky.
                          R.I.P Levelord.
