Description             : This is a small, basic ~10 minute Singleplayer Map
                          for "WAD-Mania One". Obviously, I went for a 90s
                          look with this one, like a mix of DOOM IIs early
                          levels mixed with some early 90s charm. This map
                          messes around with teleport triggers a lot; I
                          always wanted to throw some teleport traps in my
                          WADs. Anyway, there aren't many differences in
                          the difficulty settings besides the ammo pickups
                          doubling on Nightmare and "I'm too young to die".
                          there's tons of ammo scattered around (mostly
                          from dead guys) but not much health besides some
                          powerups. You probably won't have a challenge
                          playing through this besides the ending, I won't
                          spoil it, but lets just say there's a few lads
                          waiting for ya… Have fun!
                          -Arch
